# Snow Leopard Habitat Suitability and Range Shift in the Himalaya

## Overall theme
What combined effects do climate and human pressure have on snow leopards in the Himalayas?

## Project question
How is the suitable habitat of snow leopards in the Himalaya projected to shift under future climate, and does including human pressure change the projected shift?

## Status
In progress. Data collection and modelling underway.

## Planned methods
- Occurrence data: GBIF
- Climate: WorldClim 2.1
- Human pressure: livestock density, population density
- Model: Random Forest with spatial cross-validation
- Comparison: climate-only model vs climate + human pressure model

## Limitations (to be expanded)
- Occurrence data are sparse and spatially biased
- Projections show suitable habitat, not actual animal movement
